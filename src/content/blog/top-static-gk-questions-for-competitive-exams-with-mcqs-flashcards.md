<h2>Essential Static GK Master Preparation Guide</h2>

<p>Static General Knowledge (Static GK) is one of the most critical sections in competitive examinations across India, including UPSC Civil Services, SSC CGL, CHSL, RRB NTPC, Banking (SBI/IBPS PO & Clerk), and State Public Service Commissions (PSC). Since static facts do not change with time, mastering them guarantees high accuracy and boosts overall exam scores.</p>

<h3>1. Indian Polity & Constitution Highlights</h3>
<p>The Constitution of India is the longest written constitution of any sovereign country in the world. It was drafted by the Drafting Committee chaired by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar. Key constitutional facts to remember:</p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Fundamental Rights:</b> Part III (Articles 12 to 35). Article 32 (Right to Constitutional Remedies) was hailed by Dr. Ambedkar as the 'Heart and Soul' of the Constitution.</li>
  <li><b>Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP):</b> Part IV (Articles 36 to 51), borrowed from the Irish Constitution.</li>
  <li><b>Fundamental Duties:</b> Article 51A, added by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976, on the recommendation of the Swaran Singh Committee.</li>
  <li><b>Preamble:</b> The terms 'Socialist', 'Secular', and 'Integrity' were added to the Preamble by the 42nd Amendment Act in 1976.</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Indian & Physical Geography Essentials</h3>
<p>India is the 7th largest country by area and has a land border of 15,200 km and a coastline of 7,516.6 km.</p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Longest River:</b> Ganga (2,525 km in India). The longest river in Peninsular India is Godavari (1,465 km), often called 'Dakshin Ganga'.</li>
  <li><b>Highest Mountain Peak:</b> Kangchenjunga (8,586 m) in Sikkim is the highest peak located entirely within India's administered territory.</li>
  <li><b>Dams & Water Bodies:</b> Hirakud Dam (Mahanadi River, Odisha) is the longest earthen dam in India. Tehri Dam (Bhagirathi River, Uttarakhand) is the highest dam in India.</li>
  <li><b>Borders:</b> India shares its longest land border with Bangladesh (4,096.7 km) and shortest with Afghanistan (106 km).</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. Important National Parks & Environment</h3>
<p>India has over 106 national parks and 565 wildlife sanctuaries. Key highlights:</p>
<ul>
  <li><b>First National Park:</b> Hailey National Park (1936), now known as Jim Corbett National Park in Uttarakhand.</li>
  <li><b>Largest National Park:</b> Hemis National Park in Ladakh, famous for snow leopards.</li>
  <li><b>Floating National Park:</b> Keibul Lamjao National Park on Loktak Lake in Manipur, the world's only floating national park (home to the endangered Sangai deer).</li>
  <li><b>Kaziranga National Park:</b> Located in Assam, famous for the Great One-Horned Rhinoceros (UNESCO World Heritage Site).</li>
</ul>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)</h2>

<details>
<summary>Why is Article 32 called the 'Heart and Soul of the Constitution'?</summary>
<p>Article 32 guarantees the Right to Constitutional Remedies, allowing citizens to directly approach the Supreme Court if their Fundamental Rights are violated. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar emphasized that without this provision, all other rights would be meaningless.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the significance of Kaziranga National Park?</summary>
<p>Located in Assam, Kaziranga National Park hosts the world's largest population of the Great One-Horned Rhinoceros (over 2,400 rhinos). It was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1985.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How many classical dance forms are officially recognized in India?</summary>
<p>Sangeet Natak Akademi officially recognizes 8 classical dance forms: Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kathakali, Kuchipudi, Manipuri, Mohiniyattam, Odissi, and Sattriya.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which is the longest dam in India and where is it built?</summary>
<p>Hirakud Dam in Sambalpur, Odisha, built across the Mahanadi River, is the longest dam in India. Its main section spans 4.8 km, and the total dam complex measures 25.8 km.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What major institutions were established by Dr. Vikram Sarabhai?</summary>
<p>Dr. Vikram Sarabhai helped found the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), Physical Research Laboratory (PRL) in Ahmedabad, and Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Ahmedabad.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the significance of the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act 1976?</summary>
<p>Known as the 'Mini-Constitution', the 42nd Amendment added the words 'Socialist', 'Secular', and 'Integrity' to the Preamble and introduced Fundamental Duties (Article 51A) based on the Swaran Singh Committee recommendations.</p>
</details>
