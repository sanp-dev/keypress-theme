<p>Staying updated with current affairs is extremely important for competitive exams such as UPSC, SSC, Banking, and State PCS. The period between 15–16 March 2026 witnessed several major developments including the escalating Iran–Israel conflict, global oil supply disruptions, India’s energy concerns, international diplomacy, and key national events.</p>

<p>Below is a detailed exam-oriented summary of the most important current affairs.</p>

<h2>Current affairs 15-16 March</h2>

<h3>1. Iran–Israel War Intensifies</h3>
<p>The conflict between Iran and Israel intensified further as Israeli airstrikes targeted several Iranian fuel depots and oil infrastructure.</p>

<ul>
<li>Iran’s foreign minister described the attacks as “ecocide”, referring to environmental destruction caused by burning oil facilities.</li>
<li>Iran reportedly arrested more than 500 people accused of spying for Israel.</li>
<li>In Lebanon, more than 850 deaths have been reported due to the expanding conflict.</li>
</ul>

<p>The situation has raised global concerns about a wider Middle East war.</p>

<h3>2. Strait of Hormuz Nearly Closed</h3>
<p>The escalating conflict has disrupted maritime activity near the Strait of Hormuz, one of the world’s most important oil routes.</p>

<ul>
<li>Around 20% of global oil and LNG shipments pass through this route.</li>
<li>Several oil tankers avoided the region due to security risks.</li>
<li>Brent crude oil price rose to around $104 per barrel.</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. India Requests Safe Passage for Ships</h3>
<p>India began diplomatic efforts to ensure safe passage for its merchant vessels operating near the Strait of Hormuz.</p>

<ul>
<li>About 22 Indian ships were reported to be near the conflict zone.</li>
<li>Iran allowed some Indian vessels to pass safely.</li>
<li>Two LPG tankers, <b>Shivalik</b> and <b>Nanda Devi</b>, carrying about 92,000 tonnes of gas, continued toward India.</li>
</ul>

<h3>4. India Faces Possible Gas Supply Concerns</h3>
<p>Due to disruptions in Middle Eastern energy routes, India may face natural gas supply challenges.</p>

<ul>
<li>The government may prioritize domestic consumers for gas supply.</li>
<li>Industrial gas supply may be restricted if the crisis continues.</li>
<li>Officials are monitoring global LNG supply chains.</li>
</ul>

<h3>5. BRICS Coordination on Middle East Crisis</h3>
<p>India, currently holding the BRICS presidency, initiated discussions among member nations regarding the Middle East crisis.</p>

<p><b>BRICS countries include:</b></p>

<ul>
<li>Brazil</li>
<li>Russia</li>
<li>India</li>
<li>China</li>
<li>South Africa</li>
</ul>

<h3>6. United Nations Calls for Ceasefire</h3>
<p>International organizations called for diplomatic efforts to stop the escalating conflict.</p>

<ul>
<li>Global leaders urged an immediate ceasefire.</li>
<li>The World Health Organization announced $2 million in emergency aid.</li>
<li>Humanitarian groups appealed for peace negotiations.</li>
</ul>

<h3>7. Drone and Missile Alerts in Gulf Region</h3>
<p>Several Gulf countries raised security alerts due to possible spillover from the conflict.</p>

<ul>
<li>Missile and drone alerts were reported in UAE, Kuwait, and Iraq.</li>
<li>Air defense systems intercepted several drones.</li>
<li>Regional military tensions remain high.</li>
</ul>

<h3>8. International Day to Combat Islamophobia – 15 March</h3>
<p>The world observed the International Day to Combat Islamophobia on 15 March.</p>

<ul>
<li>The United Nations declared this day in 2022.</li>
<li>It commemorates victims of the 2019 Christchurch mosque attacks in New Zealand.</li>
<li>The day promotes awareness against anti-Muslim discrimination.</li>
</ul>

<h3>9. Oscars 2026 Ceremony</h3>
<p>The 98th Academy Awards (Oscars 2026) ceremony took place at the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles.</p>

<ul>
<li>It is considered the most prestigious award in the global film industry.</li>
<li>Nominees received gift bags worth around $350,000.</li>
<li>The event celebrated achievements in international cinema.</li>
</ul>

<h3>10. BCCI NAMAN Awards Ceremony</h3>
<p>The Board of Control for Cricket in India announced that several Indian cricket teams would be honored at the NAMAN Awards ceremony.</p>

<li>The awards celebrate major achievements in Indian cricket.</li>
<li>Several players will receive special honors for their performances.</li>
</ul>

<h3>11. Progressive Punjab Investors Summit 2026</h3>
<p>Punjab hosted the Progressive Punjab Investors Summit 2026 to attract global investment.</p>

<ul>
<li>More than 89 business sessions were organized.</li>
<li>The state received investment proposals worth around ?1.55 lakh crore since 2022.</li>
<li>The summit focused on manufacturing, technology, and infrastructure.</li>
</ul>

<h3>12. Papamochani Ekadashi Observed</h3>
<p>Papamochani Ekadashi was observed on 15 March 2026.</p>

<ul>
<li>The day is dedicated to Lord Vishnu.</li>
<li>Devotees observe fasting and prayers.</li>
<li>The festival occurs between Holi and Chaitra Navratri.</li>
</ul>
<h3>13. Indian Rupee Under Pressure Due to Oil Prices (16 March)</h3>
<p>The Indian rupee remained under pressure on 16 March due to rising global oil prices and geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.</p>

<ul>
<li>The rupee weakened to around 92 per US dollar in early trading.</li>
<li>Higher crude oil prices increase India's import bill.</li>
<li>Foreign investors also showed caution in Indian markets.</li>
</ul>

<h3>14. Sensex and Nifty Decline Amid Global Tensions</h3>
<p>Indian stock markets experienced volatility on 16 March due to global uncertainty and rising crude oil prices.</p>

<ul>
<li>The Sensex fell by more than 300 points during trading.</li>
<li>The Nifty index slipped close to the 23,000 level.</li>
<li>Energy prices and global tensions affected investor sentiment.</li>
</ul>

<h3>15. Weather Alerts Issued by IMD</h3>
<p>The India Meteorological Department issued weather alerts for several regions of the country.</p>

<ul>
<li>Rain and gusty winds were predicted in Delhi, Noida and Gurugram.</li>
<li>Heavy rainfall warnings were issued for parts of Northeast India.</li>
<li>Snowfall was expected in some Himalayan regions.</li>
</ul>

<h3>16. Thunderstorm Alert in West Bengal</h3>
<p>The IMD issued a yellow alert for thunderstorms and heavy rainfall in Kolkata and several districts of West Bengal.</p>

<ul>
<li>Strong winds and lightning were expected in some areas.</li>
<li>Authorities advised citizens to remain cautious.</li>
<li>The weather conditions could affect transportation and daily activities.</li>
</ul>

<h3>17. Gold Prices Show Slight Decline</h3>
<p>Gold prices showed a slight decline on 16 March due to strengthening of the US dollar in global markets.</p>

<ul>
<li>International gold prices remained near $2000 per ounce.</li>
<li>In India, gold prices remained around ?65,000 per 10 grams.</li>
<li>Market fluctuations were influenced by global economic uncertainty.</li>
</ul>

<h3>18. Som Pradosh Vrat Observed (16 March)</h3>
<p>Som Pradosh Vrat was observed on 16 March, a religious fast dedicated to Lord Shiva.</p>

<ul>
<li>The vrat occurs during the Trayodashi tithi of the Krishna Paksha.</li>
<li>Devotees perform special prayers and fasting.</li>
<li>The day is considered highly auspicious in Hindu tradition.</li>
</ul>

<script>
(function(){
  function initSmartAdviceQuiz(){
document.querySelectorAll(".qd-quiz-wrap").forEach(function(wrap) {
if (wrap._tsaInit) return;
wrap._tsaInit = true;
const options = wrap.querySelectorAll(".qd-option");
const progressBar = wrap.querySelector(".qd-progress-bar");
const percentage = wrap.querySelector(".qd-percentage");
const attemptedCount = wrap.querySelector("#qd-attemptedCount");
const correctCount = wrap.querySelector("#qd-correctCount");
const wrongCount = wrap.querySelector("#qd-wrongCount");
const message = wrap.querySelector("#qd-message");
const resetButton = wrap.querySelector(".qd-reset-btn");
if (options.length === 0) return;
const totalQuestions = new Set(Array.from(options).map(opt => opt.getAttribute("data-question"))).size;
let attempted = 0;
let correct = 0;
options.forEach(opt => {
let newOpt = opt.cloneNode(true);
opt.parentNode.replaceChild(newOpt, opt);
newOpt.addEventListener("click", function() {
const qId = this.getAttribute("data-question");
const isCorrect = this.getAttribute("data-correct") === "true";
const siblingOpts = wrap.querySelectorAll(`.qd-option[data-question="${qId}"]`);
const alreadyAnswered = Array.from(siblingOpts).some(o => o.classList.contains("qd-answered"));
if (alreadyAnswered) return;
attempted++;
siblingOpts.forEach(o => o.classList.add("qd-answered"));
if (isCorrect) {
correct++;
this.classList.add("qd-correct");
} else {
this.classList.add("qd-wrong");
siblingOpts.forEach(o => {
if (o.getAttribute("data-correct") === "true") {
o.classList.add("qd-correct");
}
});
}
const exp = wrap.querySelector(`.qd-exp[data-question="${qId}"]`);
if (exp) exp.style.display = "block";
if (attemptedCount) attemptedCount.textContent = attempted;
if (correctCount) correctCount.textContent = correct;
if (wrongCount) wrongCount.textContent = attempted - correct;
if (progressBar && percentage) {
const pct = (attempted / totalQuestions) * 100;
progressBar.style.width = pct + "%";
percentage.textContent = Math.round(pct) + "%";
}
if (attempted === totalQuestions && message) {
if (correct === totalQuestions) {
message.textContent = "CONGRATULATIONS! YOU GOT ALL CORRECT!";
} else if (correct >= totalQuestions / 2) {
message.textContent = "GREAT JOB! YOU'RE DOING WELL!";
} else {
message.textContent = "KEEP PRACTICING. YOU CAN IMPROVE!";
}
}
});
});
if (resetButton) {
let newReset = resetButton.cloneNode(true);
resetButton.parentNode.replaceChild(newReset, resetButton);
newReset.addEventListener("click", function() {
wrap.querySelectorAll(".qd-option").forEach(o => {
o.classList.remove("qd-correct", "qd-wrong", "qd-answered");
});
wrap.querySelectorAll(".qd-exp").forEach(e => {
e.style.display = "none";
});
attempted = 0;
correct = 0;
if (attemptedCount) attemptedCount.textContent = "0";
if (correctCount) correctCount.textContent = "0";
if (wrongCount) wrongCount.textContent = "0";
if (progressBar) progressBar.style.width = "0%";
if (percentage) percentage.textContent = "0%";
if (message) message.textContent = "PRACTICE REGULARLY!";
});
}
});
  }
  if (document.readyState === "loading") {
document.addEventListener("DOMContentLoaded", initSmartAdviceQuiz);
  } else {
initSmartAdviceQuiz();
  }
})();
</script>
