### Dual IP68 and IP69 Ingress Protection Standard

What truly sets the **vivo s2 specs** apart from virtually every competitor in the sub-₹40,000 segment is its dual **IP68 and IP69 rating**. While standard IP68 certification guarantees protection against static water immersion up to 1.5 meters for 30 minutes, IP69 certification goes a step further by certifying the device against high-pressure, high-temperature water jet washdowns. This structural reinforcement ensures unmatched durability against heavy downpours, accidental drops into pools, and extreme environmental exposure.

## 6.83-inch 1.5K 3D Curved AMOLED Display Experience

A high-grade display is essential for immersive streaming, responsive mobile gaming, and comfortable daylight reading. Panel resolution, peak brightness, and refresh rate dictate the visual clarity of everyday smartphone interactions.

The front fascia of the vivo S2 is dominated by an expansive **6.83-inch 3D curved AMOLED display** boasting a crisp **1.5K resolution (2,800 × 1,260 pixels)**. The screen delivers vibrant colors, infinite contrast ratios, and sharp pixel density (453 ppi), making text and high-resolution video content look incredibly crisp.

### Key Display Specifications

- **Panel Type:** 3D Curved Flexible AMOLED
- **Screen Size:** 6.83 inches diagonal
- **Resolution:** 1.5K (2,800 × 1,260 pixels)
- **Refresh Rate:** 120Hz adaptive dynamic refresh rate
- **Peak Brightness:** Up to **3,000 nits** local peak brightness
- **PWM Dimming:** High-frequency PWM dimming to reduce eye strain in low-light environments

With a peak local brightness reaching **3,000 nits**, outdoor legibility under direct sunlight is exceptional. Whether reading text under harsh sunlight or viewing HDR10+ content on streaming platforms, colors remain vivid and highlights punchy. Furthermore, subtle palm-rejection software algorithms prevent accidental touches along the curved screen edges during gaming or typing.
